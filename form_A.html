CASE SCENARIO · refer back for Q1-Q3

Mrs N - a 54-year-old patient navigating Jordan's cancer-care system

Mrs N is a 54-year-old Jordanian breast-cancer survivor in Mafraq governorate, 80 km north of Amman. She was diagnosed three years ago at a Ministry of Health (MoH) public-sector hospital, referred to King Hussein Cancer Center (KHCC, semi-governmental) for chemotherapy and surgery, and is now in follow-up. Her current routine:

  • Endocrine therapy renewed every 3 months at the KHCC outpatient clinic in Amman.
  • Annual imaging at a private radiology centre in Irbid because the KHCC waiting list is >4 months.
  • Hypothyroidism managed by her family doctor at a MoH primary-care centre in Mafraq.
  • Commercial private insurance (via husband's employer) covers private-sector care but does not cover KHCC or MoH.

Each visit she carries a paper folder of prior imaging CDs, lab printouts, hand-written prescriptions and an oncology summary. She has never seen her own clinical record electronically. Last month her family doctor prescribed levothyroxine 50 mcg, unaware of an interaction with her KHCC endocrine therapy. She noticed worsening fatigue but did not connect the dots until her next KHCC appointment two months later.

Use BOTH articles to answer Q1-Q3. The structures, sectors and HCP perspectives that produced this scenario are described explicitly in the papers.
